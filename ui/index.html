NoteshAIre

Set what you want to practise this week. Play. The bars fill from what you actually played.

For digital pianos, acoustic pianos, guitar, voice, sax and more.

  1. Connect. Digital piano: plug it in by USB. Any other instrument, or your voice: press Listen at the top when you start.
  2. Set your week. Press ✎ next to Weekly goals.
  3. Just play. Recording is automatic and starts with your computer. Closing the window keeps it listening.
  4. Name a song once when it asks. After that it knows it.

Something wrong? Fix it in Log.

Listen through the microphone?

For an acoustic piano, voice, guitar or horn. NoteshAIre listens only until you press Stop, turns what it hears into notes on this computer, and saves no audio.
